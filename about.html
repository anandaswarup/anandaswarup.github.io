About me

Researcher, educator, and mentor working on how we evaluate machine learning systems.

My research began in speech synthesis and language technologies: phrase break prediction, learned word representations, neural text-to-speech, and multilingual synthesis for Indian languages. That work, and my Ph.D. thesis at IIIT Hyderabad, taught me that a system's benchmark score and the experience of the person using it can disagree.

My recent work follows that question into generative AI. My sole-author study of large audio language models found that reference-based metrics can rank models differently from listening-based assessment. With the research teams I mentor, I study LLM judges and reward modeling, image-caption benchmarking, audience-targeted medical explanations, and the cost of efficient inference.

Across these areas I combine model development with controlled experiments, statistical testing, and human evaluation. I teach the Machine Learning track of the MSIT program at CIHL, IIIT Hyderabad Campus, and serve as a part-time Innovation Mentor at ResearchSpark Hub.
