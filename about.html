My research background is in speech synthesis and language technologies, with work on phrase break prediction, learned word representations, neural text-to-speech, and multilingual synthesis.

My recent work extends these interests into LLM evaluation, reward-model distillation, multimodal prompting, and inference efficiency. Across these areas, I combine model development with controlled experiments and human evaluation.

Alongside research, I design and teach the Machine Learning track in the MSIT program at CIHL, IIIT Hyderabad Campus, and guide research teams as a part-time Technical Lead at ResearchSpark Hub.
