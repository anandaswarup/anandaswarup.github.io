I test whether generative model outputs are actually correct, using controlled benchmarks, statistical analysis, and human review.

My research focuses on speech, language, and multimodal AI, from building neural text-to-speech systems to evaluating large language and audio models. I teach the Machine Learning track of the MSIT program at CIHL, IIIT Hyderabad Campus, and mentor applied research at ResearchSpark Hub.

Latest work · Submitted · ERC-LM @ ICASSP 2027

Reference Agreement Is Not Acoustic Correctness: Evaluating Large Audio Language Model Captions on Clotho

Testing whether reference-based metrics agree with listening when ranking large audio language model captions on Clotho. In a blind listening review, the model ranked third or fourth by reference-based metrics was rated accurate on 47 of 50 recordings.
